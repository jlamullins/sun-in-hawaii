Elsewhere on Kaua'i

    Forecasts come from the U.S. National Weather Service, not a live sky camera.
    This page pulls the NWS forecast for five points around the island from api.weather.gov. That forecast grid is about 2.5 km across and is refreshed roughly hourly, so a shower passing over your beach right now may not show up here yet — Kaua'i's microclimates shift faster than any island-wide model tracks them.
